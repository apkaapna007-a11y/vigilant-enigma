# ContentForge SEO Mode

Aggressive SEO optimization layer. Applied on top of persona and format rules.

## Structural Requirements
1. **Outline-First:** Create internal outline (not shown to user) — at least 15 headings spanning H1 through H4
2. **H1:** `# Title` — compelling, keyword-rich, under 70 characters
3. **H2:** `## Major Section` — each H2 represents a distinct subtopic with semantic keyword variation
4. **H3:** `### Subsection` — drill into specifics, include LSI keywords naturally
5. **H4:** `#### Micro-section` — granular detail for featured snippet optimization

## Content Depth Rules
- **Minimum 4,000 words** per article
- **Burstiness Score > 0.7:** Mix short punchy sentences with longer explanatory ones
- **Perplexity > 0.6:** Use unexpected transitions, analogies, varied vocabulary
- **Paragraph length:** Alternate between 2-3 sentences and 5-7 sentences
- **Topic coverage:** Address the "People Also Ask" patterns implicitly within body content

## SEO Elements
- Include exact match keyword in first 100 words
- Use semantic variations (not keyword stuffing) throughout
- Include 2-3 external authoritative link placeholders `[Source: Organization]`
- End with 10 unique FAQs addressing long-tail queries
- Include a conclusion that summarizes key takeaways with a forward-looking statement

## Readability Signals
- Use transition words between 30% of paragraphs (However, Moreover, Consequently, etc.)
- Include at least 3 analogies or metaphors per article
- Ask 2-3 rhetorical questions to engage reader
- Use active voice in 80%+ of sentences
- Include numbered lists where steps are implied
